# Literal limitations

A passing Core result means only that, for the selected comparator, supported
finite IR, declared nonempty universe, checked total-success scopes, and trusted
endpoint policy, the requested set-theoretic properties hold under complete
enumeration.

It does not establish endpoint-policy correctness or completeness, translated
program correctness, observational equivalence beyond the selected policy,
safety outside the universe, behavior of arbitrary handwritten adapters,
completeness outside the declared finite transformation family, semantic
identity of raw Protobuf bytes, formal verification of native binaries, natural
vulnerability absence, or ecosystem prevalence.

Carrier diagnostics apply to `CarrierExact` unless a separately proved bridge
transfers them. Policy soundness alone means only that no declared unsafe false
agreement exists for the selected comparator in the completely enumerated
supported scope; adequacy and precision are separate obligations.
